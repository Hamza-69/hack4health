## Decisions taken without asking

- Arabic side is the primary source for services (more complete); English values fill gaps.
- The drugs listing was taken from the English side only (the Arabic side shows the same English rows).
- Sorted listing URLs (`sort:`) are used *only* for the drugs endpoint to get a stable order; the general crawler never follows sort/filter variants.
- Facility and drug *detail* pages were crawled as extras (address, beds, subsidy %, agent...).
- CSV files are written as UTF-8 with BOM so Excel shows Arabic correctly.
